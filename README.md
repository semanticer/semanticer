## Tomáš Valenta

Android architect in Prague. Fifteen years of shipping mobile apps, the last seven at
Toggl, where I rewrote the Android app from Xamarin to Kotlin and maintained the
architecture library below.

**Right now** I'm building **7 Reads 7 Days** — a read-it-later app that refuses to let you
hoard. Seven articles, seven days each, then they burn. No archive, no extensions, no
premium tier to buy your way out. Kotlin Multiplatform: Android, iOS, and a Ktor backend.
